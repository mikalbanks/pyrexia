THE FIVE-MINUTE WALKTHROUGH
  1. Connect a source

    Create deterministic synthetic data or acquire output from the official Cortical SDK simulator.

  2. Configure an experiment

    Select stimulus response or signal stability. Inspect the recording duration, channels, and method parameters.

  3. Inspect the results

    Explore the raster, rates, channel activity, descriptive metrics, and limitations.

  4. Reproduce the result

    Download an evidence archive and verify its hashes and recomputed analysis.

  5. Discuss the next validation

    Repeat with authorized real recordings and agree on a scientific validation protocol with a research partner.

What works in this MVP

  • Published BrainDance trial-outcome analysis
  • Deterministic synthetic recordings
  • Official Cortical SDK simulator acquisition when installed
  • Recording CSV import with declared provenance
  • Provider-independent analysis and experiment specifications
  • Persistent run history, comparisons, and evidence export

What still needs access or validation

Live CL1 and FinalSpark hardware access, original BrainDance raw spike recordings, and provider-specific research permissions. Published BrainDance trial summaries are available in Intelligence. FinalSpark fixtures validate software parsing only.

No biological learning, clinical utility, compute efficiency advantage, or institutional affiliation is established by this demo.
